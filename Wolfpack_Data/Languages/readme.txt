To create a custom language pack: Duplicate the Official English pack, and replace the header and the contents with your translation.
Common problems that might cause issues when parsing your packs: invalid header, file name, missing characters (such as ', [], {} or similar), you are missing one or multiple entries, or you have the wrong file-ending.

Language packs are not supported outside the official ones and the Wolfpack Team can not, and will not, guarantee that your custom packs work or will not negatively effect your Wolfpack experience, please keep this in mind.
